Artist biography

Ariana Grande

Ariana Grande is an American singer, songwriter and performer recognised for her wide vocal range and a pop catalogue that incorporates R&B, electronic and hip-hop influences. She began her career in television before moving into music, where albums such as Yours Truly, My Everything, Dangerous Woman, Sweetener and Thank U, Next established her as a major international pop artist. Grande’s recordings often combine layered vocals, melodic hooks and modern production, while her lyrics frequently explore relationships, confidence, independence and emotional recovery. She is also known for technically demanding live vocal performances and collaborations with artists across pop, rap and R&B.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
