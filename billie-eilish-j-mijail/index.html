Artist biography

Billie Eilish

Billie Eilish is an American singer-songwriter known for close-miked vocals, unconventional pop production and a distinctive visual style developed alongside her music. Working closely with her brother and producer Finneas, she first gained attention through songs released online before the success of When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? Her recordings often use sparse arrangements, deep bass, unusual sound effects and dramatic changes in dynamics, creating music that can move between intimate, playful and unsettling. Later projects such as Happier Than Ever and Hit Me Hard and Soft expanded her approach with more varied instrumentation and vocal performances while keeping the detailed production that defines her sound.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
