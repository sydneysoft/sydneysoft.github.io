Artist biography

Drake

Drake is a Canadian rapper, singer and songwriter whose music has played a major role in shaping contemporary hip-hop, R&B and pop. After gaining early recognition as an actor and through mixtapes, he developed a style that moves easily between melodic singing and rap. Albums such as Take Care, Nothing Was the Same, Views and Scorpion established him as one of the most commercially prominent artists of his generation. His music often combines atmospheric production, personal lyrics, club rhythms and influences from dancehall, Afrobeats and UK music. Drake has also worked with a wide range of international artists and built the OVO brand around music, fashion and live events.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
