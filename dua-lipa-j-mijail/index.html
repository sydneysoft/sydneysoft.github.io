Artist biography

Dua Lipa

Dua Lipa is an English and Albanian singer-songwriter whose music is closely associated with contemporary dance-pop and disco-influenced production. After beginning her career through online releases and modelling work, she established herself internationally with songs from her self-titled debut album. Her second album, Future Nostalgia, developed a more focused dance sound built around disco, funk and electronic influences, while later work continued to explore polished pop production and club-oriented rhythms. Lipa is known for a low, distinctive vocal tone and for pairing streamlined songwriting with strongly defined visual eras. She has collaborated with artists and producers across pop and electronic music and has become a major presence in modern dance-pop.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
