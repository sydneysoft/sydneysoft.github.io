Artist biography

Ed Sheeran

Ed Sheeran is an English singer-songwriter and guitarist whose music blends acoustic songwriting with pop, folk, R&B and electronic production. He built an audience through live performance and independently released material before achieving international success with albums named using mathematical symbols, including +, ×, ÷ and =. Sheeran is known for writing songs around guitar-based arrangements while also using loop pedals and layered production in live settings. His catalogue includes intimate ballads, rhythmic pop songs and collaborations with artists from many genres. He has also written songs for other performers and developed a reputation as a prolific songwriter with a direct, melodic approach.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
