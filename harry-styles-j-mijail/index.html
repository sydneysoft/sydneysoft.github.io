Artist biography

Harry Styles

Harry Styles is an English singer, songwriter and performer who began his career as a member of One Direction before developing a solo catalogue influenced by pop, rock, folk and soft-rock traditions. His self-titled debut album introduced a more guitar-oriented sound, while Fine Line and Harry’s House expanded into brighter pop production, funk elements and more intimate songwriting. Styles is known for combining contemporary production with references to earlier decades of popular music, as well as for a strong visual identity in fashion and live performance. His solo catalogue ranges from large festival-ready singles to quieter acoustic and piano-led material.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
