Artist biography

Kanye West

Kanye West is an American rapper, producer and songwriter whose work has had a major impact on hip-hop and mainstream popular music. He first became widely known as a producer before establishing himself as a solo artist with The College Dropout in 2004. Across albums including Late Registration, Graduation, 808s & Heartbreak and My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy, he became known for changing production styles, blending soul samples, electronic textures, orchestral arrangements and experimental ideas. His music has influenced artists across rap, pop and alternative music, while his career has also extended into fashion, design and large-scale live productions.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
