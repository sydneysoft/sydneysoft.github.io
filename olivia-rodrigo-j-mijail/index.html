Artist biography

Olivia Rodrigo

Olivia Rodrigo is an American singer-songwriter and actress whose music became internationally successful through a direct and emotionally detailed style of pop songwriting. She first gained broad musical attention with “drivers license,” a piano-led single that introduced many listeners to her voice and lyrical approach. Her debut album SOUR combined pop ballads, alternative-rock energy and songs centred on heartbreak, jealousy, growing up and self-doubt. She continued developing that sound on GUTS, adding louder guitar-driven arrangements while keeping a strong focus on conversational and personal writing. Rodrigo’s music often moves between quiet vulnerability and more forceful rock-influenced performances.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
