Artist biography

Taylor Swift

Taylor Swift is an American singer-songwriter known for narrative songwriting and a career that has moved across country, pop, folk and alternative styles. She first became widely known through albums such as Fearless and Speak Now before making a major pop transition with 1989. Later projects including Folklore, Evermore and Midnights expanded her sound with more intimate writing, atmospheric production and electronic pop. Swift is also known for re-recording parts of her catalogue as Taylor’s Version releases, bringing renewed attention to artist ownership and creative control. Her songs frequently focus on relationships, memory, ambition, self-reflection and the details of everyday experience.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
