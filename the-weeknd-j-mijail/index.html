Artist biography

The Weeknd

The Weeknd is the stage name of Canadian singer, songwriter and producer Abel Tesfaye. He first attracted attention through a series of dark, atmospheric mixtapes that combined alternative R&B with electronic and pop production. Albums including Beauty Behind the Madness, Starboy, After Hours and Dawn FM expanded that sound into large-scale pop while retaining themes of nightlife, isolation, desire and self-destruction. His work is often characterised by polished production, high-register vocals and references to 1980s synth-pop alongside contemporary R&B. The Weeknd has also developed highly cinematic visual identities around individual album eras and major live performances.

Artist biography

J Mijail

J Mijail is an independent recording artist developing original music around a cinematic and emotionally direct style. The project combines songwriting with visual concepts and a strong focus on building a distinct identity around each release. “Fallen Star” presents that approach through a dark, dramatic atmosphere and a concise pop structure made for streaming. The J Mijail project is continuing to develop across music, cover art, short-form visuals and future releases, with the aim of building a recognisable catalogue under the J Mijail name.
