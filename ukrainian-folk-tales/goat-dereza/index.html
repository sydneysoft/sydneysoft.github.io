Goat-Dereza

Коза-Дереза

A man hears a young goat crying in the forest and frees her from a thicket, beginning a story about kindness, pride and reconciliation.

About the tale

The rescued goat is grateful at first, but soon becomes proud, rude and difficult. The man returns her to the forest, where loneliness gives her time to understand what she has lost. She eventually seeks him out, apologises, and the two are reunited.

In this collection

J Mijail’s version frames Goat-Dereza as a compact story of friendship tested by bad behaviour and restored through remorse and forgiveness.
