Ivasyk-Telesyk

Івасик-Телесик

A longed-for child grows into a kind and brave boy whose adventures by the river place him in danger when a hostile creature imitates a familiar voice.

About the tale

Ivasyk-Telesyk trusts the voice calling him toward shore, only to discover that he has been tricked. He must use courage and quick thinking to escape the danger and find his way back to the family who loves him.

In this collection

The bilingual illustrated version focuses on the bond between Ivasyk-Telesyk and his parents, the danger of deception and the relief of returning home safely.
