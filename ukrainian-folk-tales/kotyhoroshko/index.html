Kotyhoroshko

Котигорошко

A miraculous boy connected with a pea grows with extraordinary strength and sets out to rescue his family from a fearsome dragon.

About the tale

Kotyhoroshko follows the trail of the Zmiy into the mountains and confronts the creature directly. His strength matters, but so do loyalty and determination: the purpose of the battle is to free his brothers and sisters and bring the family together again.

In this collection

The adaptation presents one of Ukrainian folklore’s great heroic figures in a fast-moving sequence of origin, quest, battle and homecoming.
