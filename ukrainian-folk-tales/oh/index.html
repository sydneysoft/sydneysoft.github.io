Oh

Ох

A frustrated father and his lazy son encounter the mysterious forest spirit Oh, whose strange intervention begins a difficult transformation.

About the tale

The young man is forced to confront work, discipline and responsibility rather than relying on others. Through magical trials and practical labour, he changes from someone who avoids effort into someone able to use his abilities and make his own way in the world.

In this collection

The illustrated adaptation keeps the forest-spirit atmosphere while focusing on the young man’s movement from idleness toward competence, confidence and a more hopeful future.
