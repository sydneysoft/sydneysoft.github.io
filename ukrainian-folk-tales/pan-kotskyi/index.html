Pan Kotskyi

Пан Коцький

Pan Kotskyi is a clever cat living with an old man, whose quiet home is disrupted when a fox appears with tricks of her own.

About the tale

The fox tries to fool Pan Kotskyi, but he watches carefully and refuses to be deceived. The contest of wits changes into an unlikely friendship, and the old man welcomes the new companion into the household.

In this collection

The illustrated adaptation turns the encounter into a warm, humorous story about caution, intelligence and the possibility of friendship after mistrust.
