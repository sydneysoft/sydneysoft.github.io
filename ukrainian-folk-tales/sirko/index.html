Story 09 of 13

Sirko

Сірко

An ageing family dog is sent away because he can no longer guard the farm as he once did, but an unexpected friendship with a wolf gives him another chance.

About the tale

The wolf helps Sirko prove his worth by creating an opportunity for the old dog to rescue a child. The family welcomes Sirko home again, and the wolf who helped him is remembered not simply as an enemy from the forest but as a loyal friend.

In this collection

The story balances humour and tenderness, turning the familiar opposition between dog and wolf into a tale about dignity, loyalty and returning home.
