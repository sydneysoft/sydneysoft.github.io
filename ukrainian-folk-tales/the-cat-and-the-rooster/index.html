The Cat and the Rooster

Котик і Півник

Kotyk the cat, Pivnyk the rooster and Misha share a small home, but a fox repeatedly tries to lure the curious rooster outside.

About the tale

The fox uses sweet words and persistence to tempt Pivnyk away. Each time danger comes, Kotyk and Misha rush to rescue their friend. By the end, the rooster understands why caution matters and the three friends celebrate being safely together again.

In this collection

J Mijail’s adaptation folds Misha into the familiar cat-and-rooster story, emphasising friendship, vigilance and the importance of listening to good advice.
