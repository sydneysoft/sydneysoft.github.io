The Flying Ship

Летючий корабель

Misha hears that a king will give his daughter in marriage to whoever can bring a flying ship, and an old man helps him begin the impossible journey.

About the tale

The magical ship carries Misha toward the palace, but the adventure depends on the companions he meets along the way. Their unusual talents become essential when the king sets further challenges. Working together, they overcome the tests, and Misha earns the promised reward.

In this collection

The Flying Ship closes Volume I with its largest adventure: a magical vehicle, a travelling band of gifted friends, royal trials and a final celebration that brings the journey to a bright conclusion.
