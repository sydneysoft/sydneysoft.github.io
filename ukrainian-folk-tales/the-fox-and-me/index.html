The Fox and Me

Лисичка та я

A fox and the traveller Misha exchange dinner invitations, but each discovers that hospitality is not very generous when the food is served in a dish only the host can use.

About the tale

The fox serves soup on a shallow plate that is easy for her to lick but difficult for Misha to eat from. Later, Misha serves soup in a tall narrow vessel that suits him but not the fox. Their mirrored tricks expose the unfairness on both sides, and the evening ends with a better understanding of friendship.

In this collection

This adaptation places Misha directly inside the action, making the traditional lesson about treating others fairly part of the book’s recurring travel frame.
