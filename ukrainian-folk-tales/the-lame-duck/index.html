The Lame Duck

Кривенька качечка

An elderly couple find an injured duck in the forest and bring her home, where care and kindness reveal that their guest is more mysterious than she first appears.

About the tale

The duck, Kryvenka, is linked to a young woman who quietly helps the household. When the truth is discovered, she eventually leaves with the wild ducks, yet the bond with the old couple remains. She returns to visit, turning the story into a meditation on freedom, gratitude and home.

In this collection

The illustrated ending emphasises a simple idea: a true home can be a place you choose to return to, even after you have found your own path.
