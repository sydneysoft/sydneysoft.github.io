The Mitten

Рукавичка

An old man drops a mitten in the winter forest, and one animal after another discovers that the tiny woollen shelter might have room for one more guest.

About the tale

Rabbit, fox, wild boar and other forest animals crowd into the mitten for warmth. The comic tension grows as the unlikely shelter becomes fuller and fuller. When the old man returns looking for what he lost, the animals scatter back into the snowy forest.

In this collection

J Mijail’s illustrated version presents the tale in English and Ukrainian, keeping the repetition and playful escalation that make The Mitten one of the best-known Ukrainian folk stories.
