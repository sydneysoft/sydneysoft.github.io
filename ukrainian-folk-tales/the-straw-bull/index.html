The Straw Bull

Солом’яний бичок

An old couple build a little bull from straw and coat it with sticky tar. The strange creation soon becomes the centre of an unexpected chain of encounters.

About the tale

A bear, a wolf and a fox each touch the tar-covered bull and become stuck. After begging to be released, the animals promise to return with gifts. They keep their word, bringing honey, a sheep, geese and hens, and the old couple’s modest home becomes a place of abundance.

In this collection

The two-page illustrated adaptation preserves the tale’s humour, repetition and satisfying exchange between the old couple and the forest animals.
