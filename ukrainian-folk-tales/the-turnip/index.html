The Turnip

Ріпка

A classic cumulative Ukrainian folk tale about a turnip so large that pulling it from the earth becomes a task for the whole household.

About the tale

In the traditional story, one person after another joins the effort to pull up the enormous turnip. Strength alone is not enough; success comes only when everyone works together. Its repeating structure makes the tale especially memorable for children and language learners.

In this collection

The Turnip opens Volume I of J Mijail’s bilingual Ukrainian Folk Tales collection, introducing the recurring English/Ukrainian presentation used throughout the book.
